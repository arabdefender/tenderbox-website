2. What we collect

  • Contact form data — name, company, work email, phone number, and your area of interest, when you request a demo or register interest.
  • Account data — name, work email, role, company details, and login records, when you or your organisation creates a Tenderbox account.

  • Technical data — basic usage and log information needed to run and secure the service.

3. Why we process it

  • To respond to enquiries and demo requests.
  • To provide, operate, and support the platform, including sealed-bid handling, bid analysis, and audit trails.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • To send service communications, and — only with your consent — occasional product updates. You can opt out at any time.

4. No sale of personal data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your tender or bid data with other clients. Client data is used solely to provide the service to that client. We use trusted service providers (e.g. hosting) only as needed to run the platform, under appropriate contractual safeguards, including where data is transferred outside the UAE as permitted by the PDPL.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above: contact enquiries for up to 24 months after our last interaction; account and tender data for the life of the client relationship plus any period required for legal, audit, or dispute-resolution purposes; after which data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data; to restrict or object to certain processing; to request a portable copy of your data; and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@tenderbox.ae. You may also lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office.
