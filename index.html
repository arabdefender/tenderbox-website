The Current State

Once bids are opened,
the process leaves your control.

Tendering is a fiduciary process. Yet the moment returns are unsealed, most firms hand the numbers to outsiders, wait months for answers, and hope the record holds up. Four structural weaknesses, each one a cost, a delay, or a governance exposure.

01

Third parties

Opened bids are passed to external consultants and suppliers for analysis. Their existing relationships with the bidders are invisible to you, and once the numbers leave the building you cannot control where they travel.

02

Audit exposure

Analysing bids in-house raises its own questions: no separation of duties, no independent record of who saw what, and when. Either route leaves the award open to challenge.

03

Time

After a three to four month tender period, analysis, negotiation and resubmission add another fourteen weeks before a recommendation is signed. Programme cost accrues the whole time.

04

Cost

Rate outliers and missing items go undetected in spreadsheets. Value is left on the table in every negotiation, and no one can say how much.

Governance

A tender process you can
defend, line by line.

Tenderbox is built so that probity is a property of the system, not a promise from the people running it.

Sealed Bids

Sealed at database level

Submissions are encrypted and locked the moment they arrive. Not restricted by permissions: structurally unreadable until the opening event. There is no administrator override.

Controlled Opening

Dual-approval opening

Opening a tender requires two named approvers acting after the deadline: the digital equivalent of two keyholders at the box. The event, its approvers, and its exact time are recorded automatically.

Audit Trail

Append-only, hash-chained record

Invitations, submissions, addenda, openings, downloads: every action is written to an append-only log, each entry chained to the last. Amendments are additions; nothing is ever rewritten.

Separation of Duties

Roles that cannot collide

The person who creates a tender, the people who approve its opening, and the people who analyse returns hold distinct roles with distinct permissions, enforced by the platform, not by policy documents.

Evidence on Demand

The full record, exportable

Any tender's complete audit bundle exports as a single package: every event, hash, approver, and timestamp. If an award is questioned, the answer takes minutes, not a forensic exercise.

Commercial Analysis

Your QS team, augmented.

The analysis a strong QS team would produce over a fortnight, drafted in under an hour. Every figure traceable, every flag reviewable, every judgement still theirs.

  • A

    BOQ parsing and alignment

    Multi-sheet bills parsed as submitted, real-world formatting included, then aligned item-for-item across all returns. No re-entry, no transcription errors.

  • B

    Outlier detection

    Rates that sit outside the field are flagged with their spread and context, so review effort goes where the money is.

  • C

    Negotiation schedules

    Per-contractor schedules showing exactly which items to challenge and by how much: the working document for the clarification meeting.

  • D

    Award-ready exports

    Comparison tables, schedules, and minutes in Excel and PDF, formatted for the recommendation report, not for rework.

Security

Built to institutional expectations.

Short answers to the questions your IT and audit teams will ask first.

Hosting
Cloud infrastructure selected with regional data governance in mind. Data residency requirements are addressed per client engagement.
